## Full walkthrough script (~10 minutes)

### 1. Open & frame (0:00–0:45)

**On screen:** https://playin432.com/ homepage (marketing landing)

**Say:**

> Welcome — this is **Play In 432**, live on the web.  
> The promise is simple: **your music, retuned to 432** — and we mean that literally.  
> Not a streaming clone. Not a “healing frequency” app.  
> A **private player** for files you already own, with **TrueHz** retune — free for everyday 432 listening, **no account required**.

**Do:** Pause on the headline. Point to the kicker: *Private player · Free 432 listening · No account.*

**Optional line:**

> If you’ve seen apps claim they retune Spotify or YouTube audio as “pure 432,” we’re the opposite — and we’ll show that honestly in a minute.

---

### 2. How do you start? (0:45–1:30)

**On screen:** Three start cards under “How do you want to start?”

**Say:**

> We designed the homepage for how people actually start today.  
>  
> **One — Add music files.** From your computer or phone — MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A. Files stay on *this device*.  
>  
> **Two — Connect Spotify.** Important: this does **not** stream Spotify. It only reads **playlist names and song titles** so we can match them to files already in your library.  
>  
> **Three — Import a playlist** — M3U or lists you build after you’ve added tracks.  
>  
> That’s the whole onboarding story: *get music in, then retune.*

**Do:** Hover each card; don’t click Spotify yet unless you will finish OAuth live.

---

### 3. Core demo — add a track & play 432 (1:30–4:00)

**Say:**

> Let’s do the happy path. I’ll add a track I own.

**Do:**

1. Click **Add music files** (or drag a file onto the start area on desktop).  
2. Wait for import → app opens into the **player**.  
3. Confirm library shows the track.  
4. Play with **A=432** (or 440→432 free path).  
5. Briefly show source vs target / retune if controls are visible.  
6. Pause and speak over the track (or duck volume).

**Say (while playing or right after):**

> What you’re hearing is a **pitch retune** of the whole mix — ratio from concert A — with tempo preserved.  
> It’s not a pure continuous 432 sine replacing the song.  
> If you want an exact sine, we have an optional **TrueHz pure-tone bed** — separate from the music.  
> That’s the honest framing, and it’s why people who care about accuracy trust this more than magic marketing.

**Pro tip for live:** Prefer a short, clear track (piano, acoustic, or a well-known intro). Avoid long spoken podcasts.

---

### 4. Optional — Spotify match (4:00–5:30)

*Skip if time is tight or OAuth may stall.*

**Say:**

> Spotify is optional. Connect only if you want to **match a playlist you already have** to local files.  
> I’ll connect… approve the permissions… and we’re back.

**Do:**

1. Playlists tab or homepage **Connect Spotify**.  
2. Complete login.  
3. Open a small playlist.  
4. Point at song list: **In library** vs **Need file**.

**Say:**

> See the labels — **In library** means we found a matching file. **Need file** means Spotify listed the title, but the audio isn’t here yet.  
> So empty playlists after connect usually mean: *you haven’t imported the matching audio yet.*  
> We never download or retune Spotify’s masters — by design, and by platform rules.

---

### 5. Frequency story (5:30–6:30)

**On screen:** Scroll homepage to **Ten tones** / frequency cards, *or* show frequency strip in the player.

**Say:**

> Free users get the core **440 to 432** experience.  
> Lite and Pro unlock **more Solfeggio-style targets and custom A**.  
> Every card is a *reference retune* — traditional associations for mood, not medical claims.  
> We say that out loud so nobody confuses preference with science fiction.

**Do:** Click one free target if needed; if locked, open pricing instead of dead-ending.

---

### 6. What you get / differentiation (6:30–7:30)

**On screen:** “What you get” + “Not another retunes Spotify for free app”

**Say:**

> Feature-wise: live retune, private library, playlists, Learn content, HQ export with TrueHz Convert.  
> Compared to typical 432 browser gimmicks: they pitch-shift tab audio and over-promise.  
> We do **one job well** — your files, accurate ratios, honest labels.  
> That’s also how we stay review-safe and brand-safe.

---

### 7. Hear the difference (demo tones) (7:30–8:15)

**On screen:** Homepage section **Hear the difference**

**Say:**

> Before pricing — a pure A4 tone demo. Same ratio as the music engine. Headphones help.  
> I’ll toggle source versus target so you can hear the shift without a full song.

**Do:** Play source (440-ish) → target (432) once or twice. Keep it short.

---

### 8. Pricing (8:15–9:30)

**On screen:** Pricing — Free / Lite / Pro / Gift

**Say:**

> **Free forever** — unlimited live 432 listening, library, a small number of HQ exports so people can try Convert.  
>  
> **TrueHz Lite — $9.99 one-time** — all frequency targets, ten HQ exports per month. Best step-up if you want Solfeggio-style targets without going full Pro.  
>  
> **TrueHz Pro — $19 on web** — unlimited HQ, batch export, power features. For people converting albums.  
>  
> **Buy as a gift** — wellness and music communities share tools; we support that without forcing accounts.  
>  
> Restore works by **receipt email or gift code** — still no mandatory login.

**Do:** Expand Restore briefly if asked; do **not** complete a real purchase on stream unless intentional.

**iOS note (if asked):**

> On the App Store build, purchases go through Apple / RevenueCat. Web stays Stripe. Same tiers, same idea.

---

### 9. FAQ & trust (9:30–10:15)

**On screen:** Questions section (expand 1–2)

**Say:**

> FAQ covers the hard questions:  
> — Do we stream Spotify in 432? **No.**  
> — Is music uploaded? **No — on-device.**  
> — What is TrueHz? **Precision retune + Convert quality export.**  
>  
> Privacy policy is on the site for App Store and web compliance.

**Do:** Open one FAQ answer. Optional: open `/privacy.html` in a tab.

---

### 10. Close (10:15–11:00)

**On screen:** Final CTA — Add music / Connect Spotify / Open player

**Say:**

> To recap:  
> **Play In 432** is the honest 432 player — free to hear the difference, private by default, paid only when you need more targets or serious export.  
>  
> Try it at **playin432.com** — no account, drop a track you love, listen in 432.  
>  
> Thanks — happy to take questions.

**Do:** Leave the homepage or a paused player on screen. Stop talking; smile; open Q&A.

## Don’ts (live)

- Don’t promise “pure 432 Spotify/YouTube masters.”  
- Don’t open unfinished Android Play listing.  
- Don’t paste secrets or ASC keys on stream.  
- Don’t demo gift email if Resend isn’t configured.  
- Don’t get stuck in long OAuth — have files-only path ready.  
- Don’t oversell batch export unless Pro is active on the demo machine.
