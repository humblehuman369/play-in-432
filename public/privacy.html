Privacy Policy

Play In 432 · TrueHz™ technology · Last updated: July 20, 2026

Short version: Your music files stay on your device. We do not upload your library to our servers for playback or conversion. Payments are handled by Stripe. Optional Spotify login is for playlist metadata only.

1. Who we are

Play In 432 (“the App”) is a music player that retunes audio you provide using TrueHz technology. Website: https://playin432.com.

2. Music & library data

  • Audio you import is stored in your browser or app storage (e.g. IndexedDB) on your device.
  • Pitch retuning and HQ export run on-device (or in your browser). Files are not uploaded to Play In 432 servers for processing.
  • Deleting the app or site data removes the local library from that device/browser.

3. Account

The App does not require creating a Play In 432 account to listen. TrueHz Pro unlock is stored on the device/browser after a successful Stripe payment verification.

4. Payments (Stripe)

Purchases of TrueHz Pro are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe’s checkout pages, not on our servers. We receive payment confirmation (e.g. session status) needed to unlock Pro. See Stripe’s privacy policy for how they process payment data.

5. Spotify (optional)

If you connect Spotify, we use the Spotify Web API solely to read playlist metadata (names, track titles, artists) so you can match against files already in your library. We do not stream or retune Spotify audio. Tokens are stored locally on your device/browser. You can disconnect by clearing site/app data or revoking access in your Spotify account settings.

6. Analytics & advertising

The core App does not include third-party advertising SDKs. We do not sell your personal information. Hosting and security logs may be generated by our hosting provider (e.g. Vercel) when you use the website or API (IP address, user agent, timestamps) for reliability and abuse prevention.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under the age required by law in your region to consent to data practices described here.

8. Your choices

  • Clear browser or app storage to remove local library and Pro unlock state.
  • Revoke Spotify access in your Spotify account.
  • Contact us for privacy questions (see below).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: use the support contact associated with your App Store / Play listing, or the contact email published with Play In 432 / Rise In Harmony.
